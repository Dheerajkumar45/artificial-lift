Sucker Rod Pumping Systems

The global standard in artificial lift engineering. Optimize production, diagnose anomalies, and design API RP 11L compliant string architectures.

System Overview

The Sucker Rod Pump (SRP), recognized by its iconic surface "pump jack," is the most widely utilized artificial lift system in the petroleum industry. It provides highly efficient, positive-displacement lifting capabilities for a vast range of depths and fluid viscosities.

The system operates by converting the rotary motion of a prime mover into a vertical, reciprocating stroke. This stroke drives a highly engineered steel tether—the sucker rod string—thousands of feet into the wellbore to actuate a downhole pump.
